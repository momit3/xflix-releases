## What's in it

- **Your whole library**: the movies and series from your IPTV provider (Xtream Codes login)
- **IMDb ratings** on every title, and **posters, trailers, cast and plots** from TMDb
- **Top 10**, **Popular on Netflix / Prime** for your country, and **recommendations** from what you watch
- **Continue watching**, **My List**, 👍 / 👎, **search**, and filters by **language**
- **Built-in player**: several audio tracks and versions, **subtitles** (in the file or from OpenSubtitles), next episode
- **Downloads** on phones, to watch offline
- **Android TV**: made for the remote
- **Updates itself**: when a new version is out, the app offers it

## Install

1. Download the APK above (on the phone or TV itself, or copy it over).
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
3. Start XFlix and enter your **activation key** (ask whoever sent you this link).
4. Enter your IPTV login. TMDb and OpenSubtitles keys are optional, and can be added later in Settings.

After that, updates arrive inside the app: no need to come back here.
